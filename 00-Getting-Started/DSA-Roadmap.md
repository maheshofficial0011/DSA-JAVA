# DSA Roadmap

## Phase 0 — Foundations
Complexity, Java I/O, arrays, strings, debugging, problem solving.

## Phase 1 — Arrays & Strings
Traversal, frequency counting, prefix sums, difference arrays, two pointers, sliding window, subarrays and substrings.

## Phase 2 — Searching & Sorting
Linear search, binary search, bounds, binary search on answer, basic sorting, merge sort, quick sort, counting sort concepts.

## Phase 3 — Linked Lists
Singly/doubly linked lists, fast/slow pointers, reversal, cycle detection.

## Phase 4 — Stack & Queue
Stack, queue, deque, monotonic stack, applications.

## Phase 5 — Hashing
HashMap, HashSet, frequency maps and lookup patterns.

## Phase 6 — Recursion & Backtracking
Recursion, subsets, permutations, combinations and backtracking.

## Phase 7 — Trees
Binary trees, BST, traversals, height/depth and LCA.

## Phase 8 — Heap / Priority Queue
Heap concepts, PriorityQueue, Top K and median-style problems.

## Phase 9 — Graphs
Representation, BFS, DFS, shortest paths, topological sorting and Union-Find.

## Phase 10 — Dynamic Programming
Memoization, tabulation, 1D/2D DP, knapsack and subsequences.

## Phase 11 — Advanced DSA
Tries, segment trees, Fenwick trees and advanced graph/tree algorithms.

Topics are marked complete only after concepts, implementation, practice, debugging and revision are covered.
