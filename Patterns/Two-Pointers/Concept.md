# Two Pointers

Two pointers maintain two positions in a sequence and move them according to the problem.

## Opposite Ends

```text
left ->       <- right
```

Useful for array reversal and many sorted-array problems.

## Day 1 Example

Array reversal used left and right pointers and modified the array in place.

Typical complexity for this form: O(n) time and O(1) auxiliary space.
